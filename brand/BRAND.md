# InvoicePay visual identity

A folded document and outward payment arrow: an invoice that sends payment directly to its recipient.

- Primary color: `#0F766E`. Shared text ink: `#17233B`; light text: `#F8FAFC`; neutral surface: `#FFFFFF`.
- Wordmark: Arial/Helvetica sans-serif, bold, with a plain descriptive line. No external font downloads.
- `logo.svg`: light-background wordmark; `logo-dark.svg`: dark-background wordmark.
- `mark.svg`: compact 64-unit symbol; `favicon.svg`: browser icon with an opaque colored field.
- Minimum mark size: 24 px in interfaces; favicon may appear at 16 px. Keep at least one quarter of the mark width as clear space.
- Keep proportions and original colors. Do not add effects, stretch or recolor provider wallet icons.
- Use empty image alt text beside the same visible project name; otherwise use "InvoicePay". Brand images must not replace headings or safety notices.

These editable, self-contained SVGs were drawn for this project. They contain no scripts, remote references or official Stellar/Drips marks and imply no endorsement, audit, funding or production readiness. Existing repository licensing applies to these assets.
